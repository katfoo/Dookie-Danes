What is a Dookie Dane?

Through highly selective and careful breeding programs, we have pioneered the Dookie Dane. By mapping the genetic strengths of the Great Dane, Beagle, Dachshund, and Pembroke Welsh Corgi, we have engineered a dog with the profound, soulful presence of a giant breed, scaled down into an impossibly long, low-to-the-ground frame. They are excellent family dogs, requiring surprisingly little legroom.

Foundation Lineage

The Sire: "Goliath"

Goliath brings the immense bone structure of the Great Dane paired with the tri-color coat and distinct ears of a Beagle. He is gentle, massive, and highly motivated by snacks.

The Dam: "Queenie"

Queenie provides the critical low-rider genetics. With the elongated spine of a Standard Dachshund and the lush, fluffy coat of a Corgi, she is the structural foundation of the Dookie Dane.

# Majestic Dookie Danes 🐾

The official repository and web hosting infrastructure for **Majestic Dookie Danes**, North America's premier boutique breeder of the Dookie Dane. 

## About Our Program
Through highly selective and carefully monitored breeding programs, we have successfully pioneered the Dookie Dane. By mapping and isolating the genetic strengths of the Great Dane, Beagle, Dachshund, and Pembroke Welsh Corgi, we have engineered a companion animal that retains the profound, soulful presence and blocky skull of a giant breed, scaled down into an highly efficient, impossibly long, low-to-the-ground chassis. 

Our dogs are structurally sound, fiercely loyal, and require surprisingly little legroom. 

## Repository Contents
This repository maintains the frontend architecture for our public nursery announcements and foundation lineage records. 
* **High-Fidelity Assets:** Includes the Official Breed Pedigree chart and unretouched nursery photography.
* **Privacy Architecture:** Utilizes JavaScript-obfuscated contact routing to ensure client discretion and protect our waitlist from automated scraping.
* **Responsive Layout:** Built on a lightweight, static HTML/Tailwind CSS stack to ensure fast load times for our prospective families.
